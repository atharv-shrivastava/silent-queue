RT60 is extrapolated from the measured -5 dB to -25 dB decay segment (T20). A result is shown only when that segment is long enough to fit.

Important: Keep the same laptop, speaker volume, microphone, and positions for both tests. The reverberation result is an estimate from a speaker–room–microphone measurement, not a calibrated ISO measurement. Reflections, background noise, room changes, speaker response, microphone processing and automatic gain can affect it. A change does not prove that people entered the room.
